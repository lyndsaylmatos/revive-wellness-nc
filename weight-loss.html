What Is GLP-1 Weight Loss Therapy?

GLP-1 medications can support weight management by helping regulate appetite, fullness, and blood sugar. At Revive Wellness NC, treatment is personalized and medically supervised based on your health history and individual goals.

Important: We use compounded Semaglutide and Tirzepatide formulated by licensed compounding pharmacies. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved brand-name products. Your provider will review treatment options, risks, benefits, and whether therapy is appropriate for you.

Compounded Semaglutide

Semaglutide is a once-weekly injection that works by:

  • Slowing gastric emptying to help you feel full longer
  • Sending signals to the brain that reduce appetite
  • Decreasing unhealthy cravings
  • Improving blood sugar regulation

Compounded Tirzepatide

Tirzepatide is a dual-action medication that targets both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, offering:

  • Enhanced appetite suppression
  • Improved insulin sensitivity
  • Greater weight loss potential in clinical studies
  • Weekly injection schedule
