A More Personal Approach to Primary Care

Healthcare should feel personal, accessible, and centered on you. Direct Primary Care (DPC) is a membership-based approach to primary care designed to make routine healthcare more straightforward and relationship-focused.

At Revive Wellness NC in Knightdale, we are building a healthcare experience around meaningful conversations, preventive care, and convenient access. Our goal is to give patients more time with their care team and a clearer path for managing their everyday health.

Important: Direct Primary Care is a membership model for primary care. It is not a substitute for emergency care or necessarily a replacement for health insurance. Your membership terms will explain exactly what is included and what services may be billed separately.

What Is Direct Primary Care?

Traditional primary care can involve insurance billing, rushed appointments, and difficulty reaching your provider. DPC takes a different approach by using a recurring membership model for covered primary care services.

Your membership is designed to support routine visits, preventive care, common health concerns, chronic-condition management, and ongoing communication with your care team. Your membership agreement will outline exactly what is included.

What Primary Care Can Help You Manage

Primary care is the foundation of your healthcare. Depending on your needs and the services included in your membership, your care may include support with:

  • Routine health concerns and acute, non-emergency illnesses
  • Preventive care and health screenings
  • Ongoing management of common chronic health conditions
  • Medication review and management
  • Health and lifestyle goals
  • Coordination with specialists and other healthcare services when appropriate

Direct Primary Care vs. Traditional Primary Care

Traditional primary care commonly bills insurance for individual visits and services. DPC instead uses a membership model for covered primary care, helping keep the focus on ongoing access and the patient-provider relationship.

DPC does not replace every part of healthcare. Insurance may still be needed for emergencies, hospital care, specialists, imaging, procedures, and services outside the practice.

Is Direct Primary Care Right for You?

DPC may be a good fit if you want a closer relationship with your primary care team, easier access, and more continuity in your routine care.
